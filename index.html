      Are generated passwords stored anywhere?

      No. Generation is performed locally in your browser. The result is not written to a database, URL, or analytics event.

      Can I use a generated username anywhere?

      You can use it wherever you like, but MakeMePassword does not claim platform availability. Availability depends on the service you choose.

      How does analytics work?

      Optional GA4 tracking records anonymous product events such as generation and copy actions. Credential values are deliberately excluded.
